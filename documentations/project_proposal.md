# Project Proposal

## Project Title
E-Commerce Revenue, Returns & Customer Retention Analysis

## Dataset
UCI Online Retail II

## Business Context
The dataset contains transactional records from a UK-based online retailer selling gifts and other products. The business serves both individual customers and wholesalers.

## Problem Statement
The business needs to understand its sales performance, revenue trends, product demand, customer purchasing behavior, and cancellation or return patterns.

## Project Objectives
1. Analyze revenue trends over time.
2. Identify top-performing products and countries.
3. Understand cancellation and return patterns.
4. Examine customer purchasing behavior and repeat purchases.
5. Identify data quality issues and limitations.
6. Develop actionable business recommendations.
